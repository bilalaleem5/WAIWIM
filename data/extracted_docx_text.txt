For the  AI for Drug Innovation Society , I would update the website in English around a  Governance + Impact + Innovation  model rather than treating it as a conventional nonprofit website. 1. New Website Positioning AI for Drug Innovation Society Research • Innovation • Health Impact • Community The website should communicate three things immediately: We are a legally governed nonprofit organization.   We operate measurable health and innovation programs.   We are transparent to donors, volunteers, partners, and the public.   The site should therefore become a  digital governance and impact platform , not only an informational website. 2. New Main Navigation I recommend: Home About Us Governance &amp; Transparency Programs &amp; Initiatives Impact Research &amp; Innovation Strategic Partnerships Volunteer Support Us Reports &amp; Publications Contact At the top right: Search | AR / EN | Login The  Login  area leads to role-based dashboards. 3. Homepage Hero Section Advancing Health Through Artificial Intelligence and Innovation AI for Drug Innovation Society  is a nonprofit organization working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, pharmaceutical innovation, health technology, research, education, and community impact. From research to innovation. From innovation to health impact. Buttons: Explore Our Initiatives Partner With Us 4. Four Core Areas Instead of putting dozens of initiatives on the homepage, use four institutional pillars: AI &amp; Drug Innovation Advancing artificial intelligence applications in pharmaceutical research and drug discovery. Health Innovation Developing and supporting innovative solutions that improve healthcare access and delivery. Research &amp; Education Building scientific capacity through research, training, workshops, and knowledge exchange. Community Health Impact Connecting technology, volunteers, partners, and resources to address real health needs. 5. “Our Initiatives” This should become one of the most important sections. Featured Initiatives Hakeem Medical Equipment Bank A platform for collecting, assessing, maintaining or calibrating when needed, registering, allocating, transporting, and delivering medical equipment and supplies to people and organizations in need. Thousand Miles Step A capacity-building and innovation program supporting the development of future capabilities in AI-driven drug discovery and pharmaceutical innovation. AI Drug Discovery Training Unit Specialized education and training in artificial intelligence applications in drug discovery and pharmaceutical sciences. AI for Drug Innovation Hackathon A platform for students, researchers, innovators, developers, and healthcare professionals to develop technology-driven solutions to pharmaceutical and healthcare challenges. Dawā’i Bi-Tariqati — Personalized Medication An initiative focused on personalized approaches to medication and patient-centered pharmaceutical care. 6. Every Initiative Gets Its Own Impact Page This is important. Instead of simply saying: “We have this initiative.” Every initiative should have a standardized structure: Initiative Profile Problem What problem are we addressing? Solution What are we doing? Target Beneficiaries Who benefits? Partners Who supports the initiative? Funding How is it funded? Activities What has been implemented? Outputs What has been delivered? Outcomes What changed? Impact What measurable difference has been achieved? KPIs Current Status Planned  In Development  Pilot  Active  Completed  Reports &amp; Evidence This prevents the website from mixing proposed projects with active projects. 7. Governance &amp; Transparency This should be a major top-level page. Governance &amp; Transparency Legal &amp; Institutional Information Legal name  Registration information  Supervisory authority  Establishment date  Legal form  Approved bylaws  Relevant licenses and approvals  Governance Structure General Assembly ↓ Board of Directors ↓ Board Committees ↓ Executive Management ↓ Programs &amp; Initiatives The exact terminology should reflect whether the Society is legally registered as an  association  or another nonprofit form. 8. Board of Directors Create a dedicated page: Board of Directors For each board member: Name  Position  Professional background  Qualifications  Relevant experience  Committee membership  Board term  Only publish information that is appropriate and legally permissible. The governance page should also include: Board Responsibilities Board Meetings Attendance Board Decisions Committees Conflict-of-Interest Disclosure Annual Governance Report 9. Board Committees Depending on the Society's formally approved structure, the website can present committees such as: Audit &amp; Risk Committee Financial oversight, internal controls, audit, risk. Governance &amp; Nominations Committee Governance, board composition, succession, policies. Programs &amp; Impact Committee Program performance and social impact. Research &amp; Innovation Committee Scientific research, innovation, publications, and partnerships. The website should only identify committees that have actually been established by the competent authority. 10. Conflict of Interest Create a dedicated section: Conflict of Interest It should explain: Conflict-of-interest policy  Annual disclosure  Disclosure before relevant decisions  Recording conflicts in meeting minutes  Recusal from discussion/voting where applicable  Conflict management process  Internally, this should become a  Conflict of Interest Register . 11. Financial Transparency Financial Transparency Show, where approved and legally publishable: Annual financial statements  Annual reports  Budget  Revenue  Expenditure  Program expenditure  Restricted funding  Unrestricted funding  External audit  Financial policies  Significant financial developments  The principle should be: Show where resources come from, where they go, and what they achieve. 12. Donation Transparency Instead of simply having: Donate Now Use: Support Our Mission Each fundraising campaign should show: Campaign Goal Amount Raised Amount Used Remaining Amount Purpose Beneficiaries Campaign Period Impact Report Donation functionality itself should only be enabled through the legally authorized mechanism applicable to the Society and the specific campaign. 13. Donor Dashboard This should be a secure private portal. Volunteer Dashboard Volunteer Portal My Profile Skills  Expertise  Interests  Opportunities Available opportunities  Applications  Assignments  My Activities Current tasks  Completed activities  Attendance  Volunteer Hours Training Courses  Certifications  Impact Programs supported  Beneficiaries reached  Contributions  Recognition Certificates  Achievements  15. Strategic Partner Dashboard This is particularly important for your Society because of the planned relationships with universities, pharmaceutical companies, technology companies, healthcare organizations, and government institutions. The New Homepage Structure I would ultimately build the homepage like this: 01 — Hero AI for Drug Innovation Society 02 — Who We Are 03 — Our Four Areas AI &amp; Drug Innovation Health Innovation Research &amp; Education Community Health Impact 04 — Featured Initiatives 05 — Impact Dashboard 06 — Research &amp; Innovation 07 — Strategic Partnerships 08 — Volunteer 09 — Governance &amp; Transparency 10 — Reports 11 — Support Our Mission 12 — Contact Most important change The Society's website should communicate: We don't just announce initiatives.
